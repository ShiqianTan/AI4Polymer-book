                                    Abstract
                                    A semiautomated system for synthesizing polyamic acid particles using a custom liquid-handling device and a robotic arm is
                                    proposed in this study. Integration of cameras and a multimodal large language model facilitates continuous monitoring and
                                    documentation, enhancing objectivity in synthetic experiments and enabling future advancements in experimental research.
